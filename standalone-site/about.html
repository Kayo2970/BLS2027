About LEADS Next-Gen Centre

Multidisciplinary Leadership, Sustainability-First

LEADS Next Gen Centre is a premier, student-led initiative at Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences (RUAS), strategically evolved from the university's E-Cell to foster multidisciplinary leadership and a sustainability mindset. LEADS empowers students through experiential learning, ethical mentorship, and industry-aligned projects — cultivating future-ready leaders equipped to drive meaningful, sustainable impact across all sectors.

About Bharat Lead Summit

The Centre's Flagship Annual Event

Bharat Lead Summit is the Centre's flagship annual event: a strategic platform that prepares emerging executives for a resource-conscious global economy, bringing together industry leaders, policymakers, academics, and student innovators around practical strategies for building sustainable and scalable businesses.

Vision

"To empower future leaders to be purpose-driven, ethical changemakers who champion sustainable innovation and shape an equitable, resilient future."

Mission

"To empower future leaders through experiential learning, ethical mentorship, and sustainability-driven action for purposeful and impactful leadership."
